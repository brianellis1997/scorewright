# 111326: decisions for this piece only

Brian's style guide (`lab/style/brian.md`) holds general principles. What is true only of this
piece is here.

- The theme's first statement has "a slight wind feel": the oboe in its warm upper middle register
  over sustained horn chords, then the flute takes it "with the strings subtly assisting".
- The theme's harmony: one chord per 2/4 bar, a modal minor loop under a melody centred on E:
  F, G, Em, E (A minor VI, VII, v, V), with inversions in the second half (F/A, G/B).
- The introduction (S10): A minor over a bass falling by half steps, A to E, the bass of the
  lament section at bars 33-40.
- The climax (bars 48-60), as Brian wrote it after S16: the trumpet states the G falling to E,
  Violin I takes over on beat 2 of bar 52; harmony F, G, Em, A, Dm, G, C, A7 over C sharp, Dm,
  F (with D sharp: a German sixth), E, E.
